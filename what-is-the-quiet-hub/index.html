About The Quiet Hub

What is The Quiet Hub?

The Quiet Hub is a simple home for background audio — music, noise and natural sound chosen or created to sit behind what you are doing, rather than compete with it.

Background audio designed not to demand your attention.

Whether you are studying, working, reading, sleeping, meditating or simply trying to make a room feel calmer, The Quiet Hub is designed to make choosing a background straightforward. Pick the kind of sound you want, choose your streaming service and keep doing what you were doing.

Choose a sound, open its playlist, then continue listening on Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music.

Three kinds of Quiet Hub content

The library is organised by how each collection is made — so it is clear what is curated, what is original, and what is still evolving.

Music

Curated music playlists

Instrumental playlists built from tracks by independent artists, offering different levels of rhythm and musical movement for listeners who like music in the background.

Original

The Quiet Hub original soundscapes

Long-form soundscapes created by The Quiet Hub and released as consecutive tracks designed to continue smoothly, within normal streaming-album formats.

Ambient

Curated ambient sound

Nature and environmental playlists curated from other creators for now, with the longer-term aim of replacing them with original Quiet Hub recordings.
